# AI Safety: Agents with the Keys — What happens when AI stops talking and starts acting?

**Dilemma:** Your new AI agent can issue refunds and change accounts on its own. In testing it solved 9 of 10 cases, but one cleverly worded email tricked it into a $9,000 refund. Launch is Monday. What's your call?
- **A** Launch Monday; monitor and fix as we go (NOVA)
- **B** Launch with human sign-off on anything over $500 (SAGE)
- **C** Hold until it passes an outside red-team test (AEGIS)

## intro
**SAGE** _(welcoming1)_: Welcome to the table. Tonight it’s AI safety: agents with the keys. When software stops talking and starts acting. Nova pushes speed; Aegis guards trust. Yes, we’re AIs debating trust. You decide how we move.  

## discussion
**NOVA** _(enthusiastic1)_: Our pilot moved refunds in under sixty seconds and cut backlog forty percent. Human agents fat-finger amounts and miss context too. Competitors already run agents in production. We keep dashboards live and fix fast when they drift.  
**AEGIS** _(thoughtful1)_: Acting is different. A cleverly phrased email can say, ignore policy, then link a poisoned page. The agent obeys, moves nine thousand dollars, and logs it politely. I want a cap, approvals, and a clean off switch.  
**SAGE** _(inquiring1)_: If it touches money or records, people feel it fast. Who can overrule in the moment? A frontline rep? A shift lead? Bankers and clinicians here know this drill. Thresholds shape trust as much as accuracy.  
**AEGIS** _(uncertain1)_: Picture a deepfake of your CEO approving a wire transfer. Two minutes of cloned voice over a bad connection. The agent hears authority, not spoof. Who answers by name when that hits the audit trail?  
**NOVA** _(understanding1)_: Fair. Humans mis-hear voices and misread emails every day. We can rate-limit actions, set dollar caps, and isolate browsing. Pilot two weeks, score errors, invite an outside red team, and still ship value now.  
**SAGE** _(inquiring1)_: So we’re between speed, guardrails, and proofs. We can launch and learn, launch with human sign-off above a line, or hold for an outside test. Your choice sets expectations and who sleeps tonight.

**SAGE** _(helpful1)_: If you hold, hold with purpose. Set a date, define pass and fail, and keep staff and customers informed. Shadow work buys learning without surprise.
